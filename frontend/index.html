About CampusConnect

A Modern Digital Interface for College Operations

CampusConnect provides a simple digital interface for students and teachers to access and manage college information. By replacing cumbersome manual spreadsheets with an intuitive system, faculty can manage student records seamlessly while learners easily track their academic profile.

Built as a lightweight, clean, full-stack application backed by Node.js, Express, and SQLite3, CampusConnect focuses on what matters most: speed, clarity, and ease of use.

  • ✓ Full CRUD management: Create, Read, Update, Delete records
  • ✓ Clean REST APIs with instant client feedback and validation
  • ✓ Role-specific views for students and faculty
